### 🔥 [Urban Heat Island — Patna, Jaipur, Guwahati](https://github.com/Mrperfect907/Urban-heat-island-UHI-)
**Where are cities dangerously hot — and why?**

Land surface temperature derived per pixel from Landsat and read against NDVI, so heat
is explained by what's on the ground. **Jaipur** showed the most severe heat island
conditions (**mean LST 55.90 °C**); Guwahati, the greenest, markedly lower thermal stress.

- 🗺️ Output: heat-vulnerability zones for targeting green infrastructure
- 🛰️ Three cities, one consistent LST + NDVI method

`QGIS` `Landsat` `LST` `NDVI`

### 🌡️ [Urban Heat Island of Neemrana](https://github.com/Mrperfect907/Urban-Heat-Island-of-Neemrana)
**A decade of surface temperature for a fast-industrialising town.**

LSE and LST estimated over the last 10 years of the Neemrana region using Landsat 8
Red (B4), NIR (B5) and Thermal (B10) bands — NDVI separates cooling vegetation from
built-up surfaces, the thermal band locates the hot spots.

- 📦 Full QGIS project + clipped rasters in the repo, ready to open

`QGIS` `Landsat 8` `LSE` `LST`

**Scope 1, 2 and 3 — with the breakdown kept intact.**

### ⛏️ Jharia Coalfield — Mining Impact
**Is one of India's most mined landscapes regrowing?**

NDVI compared 2014 → 2024: **+0.196** net change, evidence of regeneration from
reclamation — a starting point for ground verification, not a verdict.

`QGIS` `Remote Sensing` `NDVI time series`

**Cradle-to-grave, four impact categories.**

packaging" belongs to a supply chain, reuse rate and disposal route — not a material.

`OpenLCA` `GWP100` `Excel`
